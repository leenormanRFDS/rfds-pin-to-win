Scores are held for review when pins arrive faster than a person can play, when all three pins are within a few kilometres, or when a very high score was played offline. Approving restores the score to the board. CSV includes all pages for the selected filters, with email (if provided), organisation, status and review notes. Removing one attempt may reveal that player’s next-best score.
